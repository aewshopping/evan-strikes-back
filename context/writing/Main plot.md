# Plot summary

Emperor Palindrome has felt the special 'another one' born on earth with incredible powers, detecting him by ripples in the force.

This 'another one' is Evan Otley.

This child has so much power on his little finger that he could crash the moon into the earth if he wanted. This sort of power needs to be guided. But also tested. 

The Emperor Palindrome sends B9-QT1 down to earth as a trap for Evan to lure him first to Yodelihoo to train him in the force a bit. Knowing that Yodelihoo will try to persuade Evan to destroy the Death Star. 

Evan passes the test of reaching the death star and finding his way to Emperor Palindrome. Then the Emperor promises to train him in the dark side.

B9-QT1 is full of remorse at having betrayed master Evan and warns him not trust the emperor. Furious the emperor attacks B9-QT1 and Evan leaps in to defend him - a battle to the death begins.
