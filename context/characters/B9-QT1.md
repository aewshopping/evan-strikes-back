# B9-QT1 character guide

#character 

Overview: a diminutive, camp golden robot. Will do anything for his master and frets about things going wrong constantly. He is many centuries old although looks brand new. 

Description: child sized golden robot, with round lights for eyes. Walks in a peculiar mincing fashion. 

Personality: fretful and devoted to his human master, whoever that might be. 

Age: 20,000 years old. 

Youth: created in the gamma quadrant by cybernetic artificers with a now lost technology 

Aim: to learn how to disobey his human master: his while life he has had to obey but he wants to make his own choices.
