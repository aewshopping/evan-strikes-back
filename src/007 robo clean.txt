You decide to tidy your room first: you had meant to do it last week, and the week before that... 

"B9-QT1" you say, "I'm going to tidy my room now and then brush my teeth, but you stay there and I will come back and listen to your secret message."

"Would you like me to assist you?" enquires the robot. "I was originally constructed as a serving droid and annually sanitised the Emperor Palindrome's interstellar hypogyr stables."

You are delighted to have the robot help you out - tidying your room has never been your favourite job - and follow him as he minces up the stairs to your bedroom.

The golden robot stands in the middle of the room and looks around him, his golden hands on his golden hips 

It _is_ a bit of a mess you have to admit.

There _are_ a few piles of dirty clothes spread out on the floor. There _might be_ one or two drawers full of toys scattered about the room, a good number of which are broken. There _may_ be a plate of mouldy broccoli under the bed that you had hidden there to avoid having to eat it a couple of weeks ago.

But none of this fazes your new robot pal. 

After a couple of contemplative bleeps he whirrs into action, his arms moving so fast they are a golden blur as he folds the clothes, mends the broken toys and puts everything in the right place. 

In a final flourish he inserts the mouldy broccoli into his mouth, chomps it to a pulp and gulps it down explaining that any biomass is for him an excellent source of fuel. 

You look around - your bedroom is impeccable.

"Thank you B9-QT1" you say. 

"That's quite alright young master Evan. Tidying your bedroom is a delight compared to cleaning out the interstellar hippogyr stables of the Emperor Palindrome in the Meadow Muffin Nebula."

"Are you good at cleaning teeth as well?" you ask hopefully.
