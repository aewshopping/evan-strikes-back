You decide to brush your teeth first - they do feel a bit grimy, you may have forgotten to brush them last night. 

"B9-QT1" you say, "I'm going to brush my teeth now then tidy my room, but you stay there and I will come back and listen to your secret message."

"Would you like me to help you brush your teeth Evan?" enquires the robot. "I was originally constructed as a serving droid and have am well equipped to attend to your personal toilet."

"I'm not sure I know what you..." your voice trails off as the golden robot extends a hand, with one finger outstretched. As you watch bristles start protruding from his finger. In a few seconds his finger has become one long toothbrush. 

After a brief a hacking coughing sound, he reaches into his mouth and brings out a small but perfectly formed pellet of mint toothpaste which he smears on his bristly finger. 

B9-QT1 brushes your teeth at lightening speed, his finger a golden blur as it pistons in and out of your mouth. But his touch is so delicate you almost don't feel a thing. In a few seconds it is over and your teeth feel cleaner than they have ever felt before.

Having your own robot is brilliant! 

"Now to tidy your room," announced B9-QT1, as he minces up the stairs.
