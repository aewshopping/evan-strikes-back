Not long ago, in a galaxy not very far away, our galaxy in fact, a boy woke up.

The boy was Evan Otley.

It's you.

You yawn.

You are very special. 

Today you _think_ you are very special, because it is your birthday. But you are very special for another reason too, which you are totally unaware of. 

"Wake up Evan!" yells your brother Arthur, back from Hogsnorts for the half hols. "Come and look at your presents!"

With that Arthur bounds off downstairs, and in a flash you are after after him, grabbing your dressing gown.

You run so fast into the living room that you bump into your dad who is wrestling an odd shaped parcel onto the table. 

"Happy birthday Evan!" he says, flashing his white teeth. 

"What did you get for me dad?" you ask.

Without waiting for an answer you reach out your hands for the peculiarly shaped object he has just placed on the table.

You rip off a bit a paper and something gold gleams underneath. 

In a fever of excitement you rip off the rest of the wrapping paper to reveal a shining golden _statue_, the same shape and size of a ten year old boy. 

It has golden arms, golden legs a golden body and a gold head. Only the eyes are not gold - the eyes are two pale circles made from transparent crystal.

"That's so cool, thanks dad! A golden statue. I'll put it next to my bed. Is it solid gold?"

"Well," he coughs, "not _solid_ gold no, and it's not a statue, it's actually a robot! I found it abandoned in the electrical waste dump I walk past on my way home. It's not working right now but I thought together, we could fix it up."

"I want to help fix it too!" shouts your brother Arthur.
