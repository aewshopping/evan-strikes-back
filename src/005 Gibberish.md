Being as careful as you can you gently prise open the robot's head with a cake knife. 

The hatch in his gleaming golden skull levers open on a set of tiny golden hinges. You can now see directly into what just surely be the robot's brain. 

Millions of wires, each no wider than a hair twist over and around each other. Thick multicoloured bundles of filaments fill the entire head space. It's easily the most complicated thing you have ever laid eyes upon.

You try an experimental prod with the cake knife.

Nothing. 

"Can I have a go Evan?" says Arthur standing next to you, screwdriver at the ready. 

But just at that moment a crumb of cake drops from your knife into the open brain case. It lands on a bundle of wires.

As it touches the wires it creates a blinding spark and a loud CRACK of electricity. 

You and Arthur jump back as the robot's eyes light up, even as it continues to lie motionless on the table.

Your heart pounds fast as suddenly - it starts to speak!

"Command override, command override."

The voice is shrill, harsh and metallic. 

"Bob bob bob bob bob. Forwards and backwards, backwards and forwards. Was it a car or a car I saw? Rats live in no evil star. Do geese see God? Aha aha ha ha ha ha..."

The metal voice trails off as the cake crumb disappears in a whiff of smoke.

You and Arthur look at each other, disturbed and alarmed. 

"Maybe you had better not poke his brain any more Evan," Arthur says.

You agree with him.
