B9-QT1 marches into your bedroom and looks around him, his golden hands on his golden hips 

It _is_ a bit of a mess you have to admit.

There _are_ a few piles of dirty clothes spread out on the floor. There _might be_ one or two drawers full of toys scattered about the room, a good number of which are broken. There _may_ be a plate of mouldy broccoli under the bed that you had hidden there to avoid having to eat it a couple of weeks ago.

But none of this fazes your new robot pal. 

After a couple of contemplative bleeps he whirrs into action, his arms moving so fast they are a golden blur as he folds the clothes, mends the broken toys and puts everything in the right place. 

In a final flourish he inserts the mouldy broccoli into his mouth, chomps it to a pulp and gulps it down explaining that any biomass is for him an excellent source of fuel. 

You look around - your bedroom is impeccable.

"Thank you B9-QT1" you say. 

"That's quite alright young master Evan. Tidying your bedroom is a delight compared to cleaning out the interstellar hippogyr stables of the Emperor Palindrome in the Meadow Muffin Nebula. But now: the secret message..."
