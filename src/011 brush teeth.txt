"I'm fully qualified to clean the teeth or any other body part of more than 300 different sentient and non sentient creatures," explains the marvelous robot. "Open wide please"

Your jaw drops open of its own accord as the golden robot extends a golden finger and stiff golden bristles start growing along its length. In a few seconds his finger has become one long toothbrush! 

After a brief a hacking coughing sound, he reaches into his mouth and brings out a small but perfectly formed pellet of mint toothpaste which he smears on his bristly finger. 

B9-QT1 brushes your teeth at lightening speed, his finger a golden blur as it pistons in and out of your mouth. But his touch is so delicate you almost don't feel a thing. In a few seconds it is over and your teeth feel cleaner than they have ever felt before.

Having your own robot is brilliant!
